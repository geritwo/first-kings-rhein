# 00 — Vision & victory

## Pitch
A simple 4X on a Civ-like hex map. Early Middle Ages, after the fall of Rome. You start as a
ruler with a handful of provinces among tribal territories and Roman remnants, and you build a
kingdom: convert provinces, organize the Church, bind nobles, grow towns, push the frontier.
Provinces, not cities, are the core territorial unit.

## Setting
- `DECIDED` Base model: Rhineland / Lotharingian king–count–Church triangle (Ottonian–Salian,
  ~950–1075). Culture-dependent variants (e.g. Hungarian royal county) later.
- `DECIDED` The world at start: tribal territories, possibly surviving post-imperial bishoprics,
  kingdoms and city-states in the south. See `07-world-and-opponents.md`.

## Victory
- `DECIDED` **Coronation is a milestone; victory is holding unity afterwards** (keep the kingdom
  together for N turns after being crowned).
- `PROPOSED` Coronation requires meeting legitimacy pillars: **Faith, Church, Power, Estates,
  Recognition**, then holding them for N turns.
- `DECIDED` **The crown is a movable, stealable artifact** (a map object with legitimacy value).
- `DECIDED` Primary goal: defeat/unify own-culture tribal lands, then smaller kingdoms.
- `DECIDED` Alternative goal: acquire or vassalize strategic areas (ports, mining regions).

## Feel / pillars
- `DECIDED` Emphasis on **building**: converting provinces, church organization (bishopric +
  bishop's palace in every province), local facilities as province modifiers.
- `DECIDED` Buildings use **locally available materials** (biome/culture dependent).
- `DECIDED` Every development opens a possibility and creates a constraint.

## Explicitly out of scope for now
- `DEFERRED` Succession / inheritance (maybe a random event later)
- `DEFERRED` Detailed marriage mechanics (abstract only)
- `DEFERRED` Tournaments (anachronistic for the period)
- `OPEN` Turn length / time scale
