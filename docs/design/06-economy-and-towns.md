# 06 — Economy, resources & towns

## Engine
- `DECIDED` Accumulate **point types**, spend them on local facilities that act as **province
  modifiers**; points also fund research (05).
- `DECIDED` Buildings use **local materials** (biome/culture): e.g. wood, clay/brick, stone,
  Roman spolia — affecting stats and flavour.

## Resources
- `PROPOSED` Principles: food underpins everything; every product is terrain-bound (forces trade);
  weight-to-value ratio sets trade range; deposits are hidden and finite (motivates exploration).
- `PROPOSED` Categories:
  - Food: grain, livestock, fish, salt
  - Building materials: wood, stone, spolia, clay/brick
  - Metals & military: iron, horses, silver
  - Crafts & prestige: textiles, wine, honey/wax, furs, imported luxuries
- `PROPOSED` **MVP set of 8**: grain, livestock, salt, wood, stone, iron, horses, wine.
  `OPEN` 8-product MVP vs full ~16.
- `PROPOSED` Chains: deforestation, iron-making, livestock–farming synergy, food preservation.

## Exploration
- `PROPOSED` Hidden deposits; Roman ruins offering a choice: spolia / treasure / knowledge /
  road restoration.

## Trade
- `DECIDED` Suppressing barbarians is the **prerequisite for trade routes** (see 07).
- `PROPOSED` Inter-province trade with distance tiers and route capacity limits.

## Towns
- `DECIDED` Founding a town inside a province **enables trade** and boosts some value.
- `DECIDED` Trade profit allows **steep income growth**; **special products** come from towns
  via crafts.
- `DECIDED` Town growth chain on province land:
  trade post / market town → market & staple rights → guilds → **urban bishopric** or
  **free royal town**.
- `PROPOSED` Free royal town leaves the count's province and pays the king directly in coin —
  solves the itinerant king's revenue problem, angers local lords.

## Taxation (historical reference, not yet design)
Nobles exempt from direct tax in exchange for military service · royal revenues: coinage profit
(kamara haszna), mining royalty (urbura), tolls, royal estates · peasant dues such as the ninth
(kilenced) · Church tithe. Candidates for later income mechanics.
