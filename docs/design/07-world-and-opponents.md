# 07 — World & opponents

## Start scenario
- `DECIDED` Tribal territories; possibly surviving post-imperial bishoprics; kingdoms and
  city-states in the south.
- `DECIDED` Goals: unify own-culture tribal lands → smaller kingdoms; alternatively vassalize
  strategic areas (ports, mining regions).

## Rival powers
- `DECIDED` **2–3 random opponents per game** that expand in a **scripted** way.
- `PROPOSED` Archetypes: Warlord, Missionary, Merchant, Roman Heir.
- `OPEN` Do rivals use the full relationship ladder or just conquest/submission?
- `OPEN` Do rivals conquer each other?

## Barbarians
- `DECIDED` Raiders shown on the **frontier**; suppressing them is the prerequisite for building
  trade routes and taking new land.
- `PROPOSED` Types: sea raiders, steppe riders, forest/mountain tribes.
- `PROPOSED` Per-hex **security value**; raids lower it, garrisons/forts/patrols raise it; trade
  routes and clearing require a threshold.
- `OPEN` Constant pressure vs historically timed (fading around 1000 CE)?
